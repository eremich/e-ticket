# Guidelines

<Lead>How the pieces are meant to be used. Apple HIG as the platform, a near-monochrome interface, one blue accent, and the transport code as the only other color.</Lead>

## One hero per job

A screen has one loud element. On Card and at the turnstile it is the Eticket card: balance, trips left, tap state. At a stop
it is the live arrival chip: "Tram 27 · 3 min". Lists, headers and settings stay quiet: surface on canvas, no shadows.

## Color and surfaces

- Near black and white: canvas `#F5F5F5` / black, white / graphite cards, ink text. Dark theme is true black, never blue-grey.
- One accent, `accent`: the primary button, a single selected choice and the active tab. Blue text and icons use `action`.
- One plate per interactive element. A chip that can be pressed has a gray pill; nothing inside it has its own plate.
- Independent filters (transport types, step-free) are white pills with a check, so several on at once stay calm.
- Status colors mean something: green on time or paid, yellow low balance, red declined. Dark theme uses the vivid iOS system colors.

## Copy

- Sentence case, plain words, English and Ukrainian.
- Money and arrivals first: "Tram 27 · 3 min", "₴4 left".
- Buttons say what happens: "Top up ₴100", "Notify me", "Block card".
- Errors say what happened and what to do. No apologies.

## Platform

- Large titles on tab roots, collapsing into a compact bar on scroll.
- Floating glass tab bar (iOS 26) with four labeled tabs: Home, Routes, Card, Profile. Glass only here, never on content.
- Sheets with a grabber and detents for short tasks: station, stop, top-up.
- Inset grouped lists as in iOS Settings: 44 pt rows, 17 pt titles and values, 15 pt subtitles, 29 pt icons, separators from the text, 13 pt regular section headers. Choices open a list with a checkmark.
- Segmented controls for two or three views of the same content. 44 pt touch targets. Type follows the iOS text styles in Onest (Cyrillic included).

## Adding a component

1. Props in, events out. No store access.
2. Tokens only. If you need a value, add it to `tokens.js` with a `use` note.
3. A stories file next to it: every variant and state, checked in light, dark and Ukrainian.
